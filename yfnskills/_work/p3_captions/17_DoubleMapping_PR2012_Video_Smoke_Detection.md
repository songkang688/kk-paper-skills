The start and end frame numbers of the four alarms are from frame 6 to 7, frame 19 to 34, frame 39 to 42 and frame 45 to 49, respectively. The lasting times of the four alarms are 0.08 s, 0.68 s, 0.16 s and 0.2 s. Since the alarms last for very short time, we believe that they are false alarms. Method 2 can easily eliminate these false alarms by dynamic analysis, so it does not give any false alarm. Method 3 and method 4 have no false alarm in the traffic and car light videos.
